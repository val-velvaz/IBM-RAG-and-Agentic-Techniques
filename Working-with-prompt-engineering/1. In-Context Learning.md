# In-Context Learning
It is a method of prompt engineering, consist of demostrating the task to the LLM as part of the prompt. Does not requiere additional training. The new task is learned from a small set of examples presented within the context at interference time.

## What is a prompt?
They are instructions or inputs given to an LLM designed to guide it toward performing a specific task or generating a desired output.

There are two components of a prompt:
1. Instructions: must be clear and specific
2. Context: information that helps LLM make sense of instructtions.

## What is prompt engineering?
Is the process of dessigning and refine questions, commands or statements to get relevant and accurate responses.
